# Super Mod Merge v1.3.5

## Added

- Reworked Civilian Militia's mainline infantry into **Armed Civilians**: an eight-person, pistol-equipped civilian squad with dedicated visuals and HUD icons.
- Added a spore-cloud attack for Flood Infection Forms.
- Added **Anders' Turret Drop**, which deploys a permanent combat turret that can be upgraded into specialized defenses.
- Militia technology can now unlock Shotgun Volunteers.

## Tweaks

- Rebalanced Armed Civilians with lower health, damage, and movement speed in exchange for updated cost and build-time values.
- Increased Longsword and Sabre damage, and raised their population limits.
- Normalized unit population costs, including a reduction for an affected high-population unit from 20 to 10 population.
- Improved Flood AI composition, aggression, technology choices, and build/train priorities. The Flood now build up their forces before expanding and favor Infection Form swarms more appropriately.
- Adjusted Heroic, Legendary, and Hyper-Legendary AI scaling and difficulty behavior.
- Reordered medic training commands to appear after Marine upgrades.
- Turret Drop shields are now gated behind the appropriate building upgrade.

## Fixed

- Fixed Flood AI unit production and improved Flood line-of-sight behavior.
- Fixed Condor anti-air targeting so its weapons lead fast-moving aircraft correctly.
- Fixed map-selection scrolling and input handling.
- Fixed Civilian Militia HUD icons and Armed Civilian visual presentation.
- Corrected Anders Turret Drop definitions and related upgrade behavior.
